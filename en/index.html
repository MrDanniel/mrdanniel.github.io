~/enfoque

Technology should feel natural.

I do not add features for the sake of it. Every design and development decision should make a tool clearer, more useful and more pleasant to use. If something does not improve that, it does not ship.

How I work

~/estudio

Small details.
Better tools.

An independent approach that prioritizes clarity, performance and respect for people’s time.

Lightweight by design

I prefer direct solutions, snappy interfaces and minimal dependencies. Performance is part of the experience too.

Mindful privacy

Data handling must have a clear reason. Whenever possible, tasks run on the device itself.

Useful design

Every interaction should communicate what happened. I aim for understandable, accessible and consistent interfaces.
